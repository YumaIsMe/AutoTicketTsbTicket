 Auto Ticket Creation - TSB Exploit Channel

This Tampermonkey userscript is built for the **TSB Exploit Discord support channel** to **auto click the "Create Ticket" button**, allowing users to quickly reach staff for banning exploiters without needing to manually click over 1 million times, just to get a ticket.

---

## ✅ What It Does

- 💬 **Auto-clicks the "Create Ticket" button to get you a channel faster**
- 🚫 **Stops automatically** if there are already **3+ ticket channels**, preventing ticket overload or spam(can be configured)
- ⏱️ **Runs continuously** in the background until either:
  - The button is successfully clicked.
  - Or the ticket limit is hit.
  - This means you can still play TSB meanwhile it is on, clicking the button.
